Questions about this case study

Why wasn't the clinic getting traffic online?

The business was doing well offline, but the website was not set up to rank. We started from the ground up with technical SEO and on-page optimization.

We audited and optimized the site, identified the target audience and best keywords, made the content easier to read and added long-tail keywords. Search Console shows 201 clicks at a 5.2% click-through rate.
