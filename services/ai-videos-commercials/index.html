FAQ

AI video questions, answered

How much does an AI video ad cost?

Usually much less than a filmed commercial, but it depends on length, avatars and languages. We send a fixed quote in US dollars after a free call.

Do AI video ads actually work?

Yes, when the script and offer are strong: AI makes it cheaper to test many versions and keep the winners. We always measure results in your ad account.

Will people know it is AI?

Some styles look clearly AI and some look very realistic. We avoid misleading content and follow each platform’s disclosure rules.

Can I use my own face or voice?

Yes. With your consent, we can create an avatar or voice clone of you, or use stock AI presenters.

How fast can you make an AI commercial?

First versions are usually ready in 5–7 days, and new variations in a few days after that.

Do you create AI videos for businesses across the US?

Yes. Everything is produced online for clients across the US and worldwide.

Which languages can you produce?

English and Spanish most often, plus Arabic, French, Portuguese and many more with natural AI voices.
