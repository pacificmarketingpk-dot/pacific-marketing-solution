What is an AI chatbot or voice agent?

An AI chatbot is a virtual assistant that answers customer questions by text on your website or WhatsApp. A voice agent does the same on the phone. Both can book appointments and hand tricky questions to a person.

FAQ

Common questions about chatbots and voice agents.

Straight answers on channels, accuracy, handover and setup.

What is the difference between a chatbot and a voice agent?

A chatbot answers in text on your website or in messaging apps such as WhatsApp. A voice agent talks with people on the phone. Both can draw on the same knowledge base, so customers get consistent answers on every channel.

Can the assistant book appointments?

Yes. We connect it to your calendar so it can offer free times, confirm the booking and send reminders. What it can do depends on the calendar tool you use, and we confirm that during planning.

What happens when the assistant cannot answer a question?

It tells the customer it needs a person, collects their contact details and hands the conversation to your team with the full history. We agree the rules for when to hand over before launch.

How do you keep the assistant from giving wrong answers?

Answers come from content you approve, and we set limits on what the assistant may say. We test it with real customer questions before launch and review conversations afterwards so gaps can be fixed.

Which channels and languages can it support?

We can set up website chat, WhatsApp and phone. Language support depends on the channel and the AI model used, and we confirm the languages you need during planning.

Can the assistant connect to our CRM?

Yes. Where a reliable connection exists, conversations and new contacts can be sent to your CRM so your team sees every enquiry in one place.

How long does it take to launch an assistant?

It depends on the channels, the amount of content and the tools involved. After a discovery call we give you an estimate based on your scope.
