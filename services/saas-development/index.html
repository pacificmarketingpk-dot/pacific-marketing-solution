FAQ

SaaS and MVP development questions, answered

What is an MVP?

A minimum viable product is the smallest version of your product that real users can try and pay for. It tests the idea before you spend on every feature.

How long does it take to build a SaaS MVP?

Most MVPs launch in 6–12 weeks. You get a working build to click through every week along the way.

Which tech stack do you use?

Usually Next.js, React and TypeScript on the front, FastAPI (Python) or Node.js on the back, PostgreSQL or Supabase for data, Stripe for billing and AWS or Docker for hosting.

Can you add AI features to my product?

Yes. We add Claude, OpenAI or Gemini features, AI agents, voice AI and document search where they help your users.

How much does SaaS development cost?

It depends on features, users and integrations. After a discovery call we send a fixed-scope quote in US dollars, paid in milestones.

Do I own the code?

Yes. You get full IP transfer: clean source code, documentation and access to every cloud account.

Can you take over a product another developer started?

Yes. We review the existing code first, tell you honestly what to keep or rebuild, then continue in milestones.
